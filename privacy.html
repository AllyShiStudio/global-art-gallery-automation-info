Privacy policy

This document describes Global Art Gallery Automation, a desktop workflow for its channel owner. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

The workflow accesses the authorized YouTube channel identity, video metadata and statuses, and channel analytics. It uploads owner-selected video files and metadata to YouTube. It uses channel information to avoid duplicate uploads, track processing and publication, and inform future content preparation.

OAuth credentials and upload-session records are stored locally in a restricted credential directory. Credentials are excluded from project logs and cloud document synchronization. Operational receipts and analytics snapshots are stored in the local project; selected reports may be synchronized to the owner’s connected Google Drive.

The workflow is operated through Codex and uses Google’s YouTube and Analytics services. Operational context is processed through these services as needed to perform the owner’s instructions. This application does not sell Google user data or provide an advertising-data service.

The owner can revoke access through Google Account third-party connections and remove locally stored credentials. Revocation stops future API access; it does not delete videos already uploaded or previously saved reports. Reports and credentials can be removed by the owner from their storage locations.

This workflow’s use and transfer of Google API information will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

For requests about this owner-operated application, use the contact details on its Google authorization screen.
